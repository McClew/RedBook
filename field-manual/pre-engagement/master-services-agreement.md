# Master Service Agreement

The MSA is the high-level contract that governs the overall ongoing relationship between the pentesting firm and the client.

* **Purpose**: establishes the legal and business terms.
* **Scope**: It is broad and does not cover specific technical details of a single test.
* **Frequency**: Signed once and applies to all future engagements (unless nullified).
* **Key Components**:
  * **Liability & Confidentiality**: Defines non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) and liability limits.
  * **Payment Terms**: Often set to **Net 30** (the client has 30 days to pay after the invoice is received). In the instructor's model, invoicing occurs upon delivery of the report.
  * **Insurance**: Independent testers must carry insurance to touch client infrastructure. Common requirements include:
    * Commercial General Liability.
    * Professional Liability.
    * Cyber Insurance (often up to $1 million coverage).
  * **Intellectual Property Rights**: Clarifies ownership of the findings and reports.
